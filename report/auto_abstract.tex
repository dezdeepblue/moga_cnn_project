We optimize CNN design as a three-objective problem: maximize CIFAR-10 validation accuracy while minimizing parameters and FLOPs. Using NSGA-II, we evolve a Pareto front of trade-offs. The best discovered proxy-validation accuracy is 0.5600. The smallest model has 29,738 parameters; the lowest-FLOPs model requires 29,721,536 FLOPs. Across seeds, hypervolume projections and IGD quantify run-to-run stability. Selected models are retrained on CIFAR100 to assess generalization.
